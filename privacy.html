Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

bachata.video is a simple music video site. We collect as little data as possible and rely only on privacy-friendly third-party services. Here is exactly what happens when you visit.

Analytics

We use Fathom Analytics to understand how the site is used. Fathom does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. Your IP address is processed only briefly and never stored. We cannot identify you individually from anything Fathom collects.

Cookies

We store a single cookie (hasConsent) to remember that you have accepted this privacy policy and the YouTube embed. This cookie contains no personal information and expires after 12 months. No other cookies are set by bachata.video itself.

YouTube

The video player is powered by the YouTube IFrame API, served from youtube-nocookie.com to minimise tracking. Once you accept the cookie banner, YouTube may set its own cookies and collect data according to its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. We do not run advertising. We do not sell or share any data with third parties beyond Fathom Analytics and YouTube as described above.

Your rights

You can reset your cookie consent at any time using the "Reset cookies" link in the player dock, which clears the hasConsent cookie and reloads the page. If you have questions about data held by YouTube or Fathom, please refer to their respective privacy policies.
